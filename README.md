# Rebound — Blade & Sorcery Mod

Throw any item, press grip on an empty hand, and it flies back to you Mjolnir-style — arcing overhead, auto-orienting, and landing right in your grip.

## How to Use

1. Throw any item
2. Hold grip on your empty hand
3. The item arcs back and snaps into your grip

## Install

Drop the `Rebound` folder into your mods directory:

`<game>\BladeAndSorcery_Data\StreamingAssets\Mods\`
